WELCOME TO THE FLOATING SETTLEMENT

Five mouths, one small island.

The forest grows even when you are away. Keep the settlers fed by planting crops on grass or reclaimed thickets, then let the days pass.

  • Grain grows quickly; roots endure frost; berries take longer but yield more.
  • Every fifth dusk, settlers collect food. The first target is 55 food; demand grows by 12 each cycle. Shortages lower hope.
  • Nature spreads into open ground. Cut back brambles or let the wilderness flourish. Rain helps growth, frost slows it.
  • Tap to plant; harvests earn food and seeds. Spend seeds on Improvements to grow and collect faster. Drag or use ↶ ↷ to rotate; wheel, pinch or + / − to zoom.
