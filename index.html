WELCOME TO THE FLOATING SETTLEMENT

Five mouths, one small island.

The forest grows even when you are away. Keep the settlers fed by planting crops on grass or reclaimed thickets, then let the days pass.

  • Grain grows quickly; roots endure frost; berries take longer but yield more.
  • Start with 5 settlers. Each eats 2 food every game-day, after that day's harvest. The five-day requirement is a forecast; there is no extra ration bill.
  • Missing food costs up to 15 hope per day, proportional to the shortfall, rounded up; hope never falls below 5. A fully fed day restores 1 hope. No deaths or food debt.
  • Every five completed days, one settler may join if everyone was fed throughout that period, hope is at least 70, and stored food covers five days for the larger population. New arrivals eat starting the following day. No food is charged for arrival.
  • Nature spreads into open ground. Rain helps growth, frost slows it. Open the settlement badge for food supply and a five-day forecast at the current population.
  • Tap to plant; harvests earn food and seeds. Spend seeds on Improvements to grow and collect faster. Drag or use ↶ ↷ to rotate; wheel, pinch or + / − to zoom.
